Mode: Operate. Audience: authenticated Student, Support, or Admin. Task: manage personal profile, email proof, and sign-in methods inside the existing role-specific Settings pages. There is no separate `/conta` route or redirect. Constraints: preserve role-specific authorization; a Student whose platform access is suspended cannot enter any internal route, including Settings; no email change until both addresses are proven; email change does not require recent-session reauthentication; confirmation links never create sessions; private avatar objects are scoped to the signed-in user. Build path: code-led because repository instructions prohibit opening local URLs or visual previews.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One calm, trustworthy personal-account group inside each role's existing Settings page, distinct from learning privacy preferences and product-wide controls.
OWN-WORLD: Extend the existing dark-only NeuroCapacitar system with its comfortable spacing, Geist typography, semantic teal/sage surfaces, orange reserved for meaningful emphasis, PageContainer, PageHeader, Cards, Fields, Alerts, and existing control states.
STORY: Identify the account and verification state first; edit name/avatar, understand connected Google/password methods, then verify or safely change email.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Settings page title remains primary. Student has no tabs or Scrollspy: Profile, available login methods and Privacy sections are vertically stacked in a centered `max-w-4xl` column. Admin/Support with `viewSettings` use three broad tabs styled like the Aula editor: `Perfil`, `Certificados` and `Plataforma`; login methods live in Profile, and Plataforma stacks login-screen media, banners and FAQ. The full Admin/Support settings page is centered at `max-w-4xl`. Staff without `viewSettings` see the personal profile directly, without a one-tab or two-tab bar. Keep user-menu links pointed to the profile section; map the legacy `?tab=acesso` route to `Perfil` and old platform-tab query values to `Plataforma`. Do not create nested field columns or duplicate email summaries/descriptions.
FORM: The Profile section begins with `Minha conta` and a short subtitle; its first group has one `Informações pessoais` Card, with no divider between the full-width name input and read-only current email. The name field's `Usado em novos certificados` helper is omitted. Name and email use InputGroup with leading icons; `Salvar` and `Alterar` sit beside, not inside, the groups. `Salvar` stays disabled until the normalized name differs from the saved value, while a pending save blocks repeat submission. Email editing opens a compact dialog. Confirmed email uses the custom filled circle-check inside the input's trailing addon, with a `Confirmado` tooltip on that icon only; the icon remains keyboard-focusable and named for assistive technology. Unconfirmed email retains a warning label and its confirmation action. The personal-data group and `Métodos de entrada` are separated by a comfortable vertical gap; headings remain close to their own content. Avatar selection remains available by clicking the 64px image; `Enviar avatar` is the primary action and conditional `Remover` is destructive, with clear space between them. Do not show a permanent format/size helper below the avatar; validation errors explain rejected files. Initials are automatic fallback, never a selectable mode. A compact Google card sits in the same Profile section under `Métodos de entrada`; no separate Acesso tab. Settings never create or change password. Password recovery remains the email-link flow from `/entrar` via `/recuperar-senha`; do not duplicate it in Settings. Provider linking is not described as profile synchronization. Student privacy preferences are a vertically stacked section with its existing Card; global Hub settings remain protected in Admin/Support tabs. A suspended Student is redirected to the blocked state at `/entrar`; no profile, avatar, privacy or other internal settings are available. No dedicated account route.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
